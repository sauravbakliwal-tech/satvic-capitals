Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Satvic Capitals Private Limited ("Satvic Capitals", "we", "us") operates satviccapitals.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Site, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what you choose to share with us through the enquiry form or WhatsApp, which may include:

  • Your name and phone number
  • The counter(s) you are enquiring about
  • Any additional details you include in your message

We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party advertising scripts on this Site at present. If that changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and share relevant information or documentation
  • To follow up on a request you have initiated
  • To maintain an internal record of client communications

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. How Information Is Shared

When you submit the enquiry form, your details are transmitted directly to our WhatsApp number via a standard WhatsApp deep link. No information passes through, or is stored on, any third-party server operated by us. We do not share your information with unaffiliated third parties except where required to fulfil your specific request (for example, connecting you with documentation relating to a counter you've enquired about) or where required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry-related communications for as long as reasonably necessary to service your request and maintain appropriate business records, after which they may be deleted.

6. Your Rights

You may request details of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or request its deletion, by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 99303 16782.

7. Third-Party Links

The Site may reference third-party platforms or companies for informational purposes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party website you may visit.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact Us
