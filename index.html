Our Approach

A private market deserves a private-banking standard of care.

Most of India's fastest-growing enterprises trade privately for years before any public listing. We exist to make that period navigable — with the same rigour investors expect on the public markets.

01 — Timing

Position ahead of the listing cycle

Evaluate a business while it is still private, ahead of the valuation reset that a public listing often brings.

02 — Diligence

Research over rumour

Coverage is checked against filings, sector data, and desk-level deal flow — not grey-market chatter alone.

03 — Discretion

A named point of contact

No anonymous order books. Every enquiry is handled directly by our desk, with full documentation support.

The case for The case for caution
Earlier exposure

Entry ahead of the listing cycleInvestors can gain exposure to a business before a public listing re-prices it.

Liquidity

No continuous marketExiting a position depends on finding a willing counterparty; there is no order book.

Diversification

Lower correlation to index sentimentUnlisted holdings don't move with daily market-wide swings, which can diversify a broader portfolio.

Pricing

Wider, indicative spreadsPrices are privately negotiated and can vary meaningfully between platforms and desks.

Scarce access

Names with no listed equivalentSome businesses — exchanges, large private groups — simply aren't available any other way.

Disclosure

Lighter reporting standardsUnlisted companies aren't bound by exchange disclosure norms, so information is comparatively thin.

Negotiated entry

Pricing set by discussion, not momentumA patient buyer isn't competing against order-book sentiment for their entry price.

Time horizon

No fixed holding periodMany positions are held for years awaiting a listing or strategic sale, with no guaranteed timeline.
